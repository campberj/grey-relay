## Missions and timeline

### M001 SILENT RESIDENT — CLOSED / FAILED

**Intent:** discreetly contact an informant at Habitat Nine, recover person/data if possible, extract without detection.

**Deployment:** WO Ana Varga (VEIL, Covert Handler) approaches Safe Flat from south; Boone Marksman Team provides rooftop overwatch; Boone Recon Team held as QRF about 700 m off-table. Original clock starts ~1930Z. The mission took place on a 3 ft x 3 ft tabletop with six rounds planned.

**Opposing force:** initial UN Reserve Fireteam security patrol and UN Pointmen controlling the vicinity; additional Reserve QRF arrived east at Round 5. Specific model counts followed player's actual fielded composition; avoid merging distinct Reserve Fireteam cards.

**Engagement record:**
1. A **Local Watchers** mission event initiated/accelerated Authority alert; VEIL engaged from the Safe Flat and killed two riflemen with a grenade.
2. During Round 3, Pointmen fire downed Marksman Team spotter **Specialist Nia Calder** on a separate western rooftop.
3. By bottom of Round 4, Reserve rifle fire through lower windows hit VEIL; her armor failed and she was incapacitated. She remained in the Flat.
4. Friendly Recon QRF arrived after the call and conducted a counterattack; SAW gunner killed the two barricade defenders, then engaged the arriving eastern QRF and downed an additional rifleman before being hit.
5. Another Boone Recon rifleman destroyed one Pointman; a remaining Pointman missed at Recon Team Leader.
6. Recon Team Leader physically recovered and evacuated the SAW gunner; surviving Recon withdrew **south**. Surviving Marksman Cpl Tomas Venn withdrew **west**, without spending actions to recover Calder.
7. Battle ended without contacting the informant or obtaining data; the GM ordered withdrawal rather than an unsupported rescue assault.

**Recorded adversary losses:** five UN riflemen and one Pointman. These are tabletop OOA results, not necessarily permanently dead in their own campaign medical system.

### Fault analysis from M001

**Scenario design defect — stealth feasibility:** Pointmen deployed within 4 inches of the informant Flat; informant contact required no enemies within 6 inches. Their assigned AI guarded the Flat, making stealth success implausible.

**Scenario design defect — watcher:** random Local Watchers escalated immediately, without a suspicious-contact or evasion phase. The user was denied meaningful options to manage civilian exposure.

**Future acceptance rule:** every stealth scenario must be playtested with a valid noncombat success path, separate suspicion from full alarm, and offer observable warnings, agency or a contingency. No retroactive edits to M001.
